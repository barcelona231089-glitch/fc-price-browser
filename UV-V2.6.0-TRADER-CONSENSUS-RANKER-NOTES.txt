FC ÜV Brain v2.6.0 — Trader Consensus Ranker

Goal
- Make the 100-card list look more like a serious current endgame trader portfolio and less like a budget-filler list.
- Use the strongest public trading ideas without copying proprietary code, paid lists or hidden player picks.
- Keep stability and existing safety behavior more important than adding fragile external dependencies.

Public ideas translated into the ranker
1. Futpepi-style workflow: requested total budget -> complete 100-slot list; budget fit is explicit, but cheap filler does not win by price alone.
2. FUT.GG usage/momentum: real community/pro usage and momentum are the heaviest public demand evidence already available to the Brain.
3. FUTZIP-style market thinking: prefer stable, active prices and punish falling/erratic cards. v2.6 implements this from our own PostgreSQL history, price activity, trend and stability instead of adding a brittle new scraper.
4. FutStarz-style demand windows: promo/in-packs/momentum can raise a card only when demand/sellability supports it.
5. Public popularity is a tie-breaker, never a substitute for live price/demand evidence.

Trader Consensus score
- Demand component: gameplay/popularity + sale-likelihood + turnover + FUT.GG usage.
- Market component: PostgreSQL stability + price activity + trend + data confidence, with supply penalty.
- Promo component: promo-market score + demand + sale-likelihood + momentum.
- Budget fit, conservative profit quality and confidence remain explicit components.
- Hard risk/capital-lock penalties remain active.

Endgame rules at 300k / 100 slots
- Max 2 normal 82-rated cards.
- Max 8 normal 82+83-rated cards combined.
- Normal 84+ is strongly preferred when safe and affordable.
- FUTTIES/Specials get a boost only when demand-backed.
- Exact duplicates remain unique-first with existing max-two fallback only when mathematically necessary.

Safety
- No new external service is required at runtime.
- No private Futpepi/FutStarz/other paid player picks are imported.
- No invented sales-per-day or sale probability.
- Bronze, sub-82 normal cards, weak low-rated Non-Rares, crash/source-risk and existing lifecycle/safety guards remain authoritative.
- Live recheck remains mandatory before buying.

Diagnostics
- Adds per-card Trader Consensus score and tags.
- Adds average Trader Consensus score to portfolio summary.
- Existing Trader-Endgame diagnostics remain for comparison.

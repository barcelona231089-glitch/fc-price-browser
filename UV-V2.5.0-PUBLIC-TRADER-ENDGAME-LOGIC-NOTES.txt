FC ÜV Brain v2.5.0 — Public Trader Endgame Logic

Goal
- Improve the actual 100-card recommendations at the end of FC26.
- Stop using normal 82/83 cards as cheap filler when higher-rated or liquid special cards are available.
- Borrow only publicly documented ÜV methodology, not proprietary code or hidden/premium player picks.

Public methodology translated into the ranker
1. Demand/popularity first: live FUT.GG Most Used, Momentum, sale-likelihood and activity remain stronger than static priors.
2. Specials are preferred when they are liquid. FUTTIES gets an extra boost only when backed by demand, usage, momentum or sale-likelihood.
3. Higher-rated normal cards receive an endgame preference; normal 82/83 cards receive an endgame ranking penalty.
4. Popular league/nation is only a small tie-breaker, never a replacement for live demand evidence.
5. Total budget, hard 100 slots, EA tax, source-risk, crash guards, Bronze block, sub-82 normal block and Non-Rare gates remain authoritative.

Endgame portfolio caps (100 slots)
- Budget/slot >= 2,500 coins (e.g. 300k/100 and higher): max 4 normal 82s; max 12 normal 82+83 combined.
- Budget/slot 1,500-2,499: max 8 normal 82s; max 20 normal 82+83 combined.
- Budget/slot 900-1,499: max 16 normal 82s; max 35 normal 82+83 combined.
- Lower budgets: relaxed caps to preserve feasibility.
- Specials do not count against these low-normal-card caps.

Rebalance
- Old saved lists are not allowed to keep unlimited 82/83 KEEP/REPRICE slots in ENDGAME.
- Excess low normal cards are released progressively and replaced through the same safe candidate pipeline.

Diagnostics/UI
- Exposes FUTTIES count, normal 82 count, normal 83 count, normal 84+ count, average Trader-Endgame score and active low-rating caps.
- Each card detail can show Trader-Endgame score and the strongest public-method tags.

Safety/accuracy
- No Futpepi source code was copied.
- No paid/private Futpepi list or hidden player picks were imported.
- Public trader methodology is a ranking prior only. Live FUT.GG evidence and the existing safety pipeline remain the decision base.

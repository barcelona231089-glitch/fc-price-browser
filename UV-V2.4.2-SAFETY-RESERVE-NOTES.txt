FC ÜV Brain v2.4.2 — 82+ Budget Safety Reserve

Observed live issue
- v2.4.1 correctly isolated the Top-100 score from the safety selection score, but a real 300k generation still stopped at 93/100 affordable positions.
- The remaining bottleneck was the sellability fallback itself: average but still hard-safe 82+ Base Rares could be filtered out before the budget Top-100 optimizer compared them.

Fix
- Adds a final Budget Safety Reserve after the existing strict and budget-adaptive sellability ladders.
- Hard safety remains mandatory: no Bronze, no normal card below 82, low-rated Non-Rare rules remain, severe price crash/source divergence remains blocked, and reported REJECT outcomes remain blocked.
- Average-but-safe 82+ Rares can enter the final candidate universe, where budgetTop100Score ranks them instead of using a pre-ranking demand cutoff as a hard admission rule.
- This reserve is only used when the existing pool cannot mathematically produce all 100 slots inside the user's budget.

Diagnostics
- The 100-Slot-Guard now reports the Safety-Reserve stage if even the reserve cannot reach 100.
- A missing minimum-cost estimate no longer displays as “0 Coins”.

Unchanged
- saved PostgreSQL lists and reopen flow
- persisted live KEEP/REPRICE/WAIT/DROP state
- snapshot recovery/defer from v2.3.6
- normal-card 82 floor
- Bronze hard block
- max 2 exact copies / max 3 slots per player
- HA, Discord, PostgreSQL and Trader Brain server code

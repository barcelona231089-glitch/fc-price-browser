FC ÜV Brain v2.7.0 — Budget Tier Allocator

Goal
- Keep the user's established rule: exactly 100 actual portfolio slots when the market can safely support them.
- Stop treating higher budget as a simple minimum-rating problem.
- Separate the broad quality candidate pool from the final 100-slot capital allocation.
- Treat low-rated SPECIAL versions differently from low-rated NORMAL cards.

What changed
1. Budget -> price-tier shape -> quality cards
   - 500k, 1m, 3m and 12m observed FutStarz list shapes are encoded only as SOFT price-distribution priors.
   - They are never a live price source and never imported as player picks.
   - All price tiers are normalized back to the user's real total budget, so the allocator does not blindly copy an external list's spend.

2. Candidate pool and allocation are now explicit separate concepts
   - Candidate pool: broad safe universe inside a budget-aware price window.
   - Allocation: optimizer chooses exactly 100 actual slots under the total budget.
   - Existing max two exact copies and max three slots per footballer remain unchanged.

3. Card version class is explicit
   - SPECIAL/NORMAL is taken from the item record/card type, with EA item id remaining the card identity.
   - A low-rated SPECIAL is not treated as cheap normal-gold filler merely because of its rating.
   - Normal-card hard floors, Bronze block, Non-Rare gates and Endgame 82/83 caps remain authoritative.

4. New Budget Tier score
   - Price-tier fit to the requested budget.
   - FUT.GG-backed demand/sellability/usage/momentum.
   - PostgreSQL stability/activity/trend.
   - Conservative net-profit quality after 5% EA tax.
   - Data confidence and risk/capital-lock penalties.
   - Oversized single positions get a soft concentration penalty unless demand is exceptionally strong.

5. Special-card mix adapts upward with budget
   - The special target remains soft, never a quota.
   - Higher budgets can naturally become much more Special-heavy when the live candidate universe supports it.
   - Safety and budget feasibility always override the target.

Reference profiles
- 500k shape: broader cheap/core/special range.
- 1m shape: tighter core around the observed low-cost Special tier.
- 3m shape: wider mixed price ladder, including lower-rated Special versions when demand supports them.
- 12m shape: broad premium ladder with concentration control.

Important non-goals
- No FutStarz credentials, API, automation or bot integration.
- No Futpepi/FutStarz proprietary code or paid player picks.
- No invented sales/day or general sale probability.
- No blind copying of FutStarz displayed prices.
- FUT.GG remains the live market anchor; PostgreSQL remains the history/stability layer.

Safety preserved
- Bronze hard block.
- Normal cards below 82 blocked.
- Low-rated Non-Rare gates unchanged.
- Sub-82 Specials require strong verified demand under the existing pipeline.
- Crash/source-risk/lifecycle/reported-outcome guards unchanged.
- Max two exact copies only as feasibility fallback.
- Live Recheck remains mandatory before buying.

Diagnostics added
- Budget Tier score per card.
- Budget Tier profile and band.
- SPECIAL/NORMAL version class.
- Budget Tier tags and price-ratio fit.
- Portfolio average Budget Tier score.

Verification
- 129/129 automated tests passed.
- 31/31 JS/MJS syntax checks passed.
- Synthetic allocator checks passed for 100k, 300k, 500k, 1m, 3m and 12m with exactly 100 slots and total buy <= budget.

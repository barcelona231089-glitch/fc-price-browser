FC ÜV Brain v2.4.1 — Top-100 Safety Isolation

Problem fixed
- v2.4.0 blended budgetTop100Score into selectionScore before the candidate safety pipeline.
- On the live 300k market this could shrink the safe pool and trigger the 100-Slot-Guard even though v2.3.6 had enough budget-feasible safe candidates.

Fix
- selectionScore is again the original v2.3.6 safety/quality score.
- budgetTop100Score remains a separate ranking signal.
- The final optimizer already reads budgetTop100Score when choosing among safety-approved candidates, so Top-100 behavior remains without changing admission thresholds.
- Live recheck and rebalance also keep the safety selection score isolated.

Unchanged
- saved PostgreSQL lists and reopen flow
- persisted live KEEP/REPRICE/WAIT/DROP state
- normal cards below 82 blocked
- bronze hard block
- max 2 exact copies / max 3 slots per player
- snapshot recovery/defer behavior from v2.3.6
- HA, Discord and Trader Brain server code
